## How priorities are assigned

Priority measures how useful a source is for this specific Seattle career, AI, and technical-event pull. It is not a judgment about the quality of the organizer or its events. Sources receive higher priority when they consistently provide:

- Seattle-area or clearly relevant online events.
- A strong AI, software-engineering, data, infrastructure, or technical-career focus.
- Publicly accessible event and registration details.
- Reliable organizer-level information that can be verified and deduplicated efficiently.

The routine weekly pull includes active sources with priority 80 or higher. Lower-priority sources remain documented for occasional discovery and manual review.

## Source rules

- Prefer the official organizer or registration page for event details.
- Never infer missing dates, times, locations, or registration status.
- Deduplicate by event title, date, and organizer—not URL alone.
- Update this file when a source is added, removed, or reprioritized.
