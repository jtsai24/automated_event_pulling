## Conference rules

- Prefer official conference, organizer, association, or venue pages.
- Avoid generic conference-alert directories as primary evidence.
- Deduplicate by conference name, date, and organizer.
- Add verified conferences to the same Notion event database for review.
- Capture registration deadlines, pricing, approval requirements, venue, and published schedules.
- Do not register or make attendance decisions automatically.
